Inkbound Rooftops V10: ORIGINAL SOUND EFFECTS

20 original, procedural game sound effects authored specifically for this source upgrade.
No third-party audio recordings and no copyrighted music used.
All samples are uncompressed 16-bit PCM mono WAV, 48,000 Hz.
The Android AAudio mixer decodes them into RAM and streams stereo PCM.

Effects: footstep_roof, jump, wall_jump, landing_hard, dash,
sword_light, sword_heavy, dimensional_rift, slash_impact,
paint_stroke, paint_platform, glyph_activated, ui_tick,
hook_fire, hook_attach, hook_release, hook_miss, hook_reel,
stone_crack, debris_fall.

To regenerate: python3 scripts/generate_original_sfx.py
(Regeneration requires numpy and scipy, NOT required to build/run the game.)

Audio attribution: original synthesized assets created for this project.
